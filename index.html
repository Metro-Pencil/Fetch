Setup notes
  1. Your computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the ESP32.
  2. Find the device IP from the Serial Monitor on boot.
  3. For the upload result to show correctly here (not just on the device), add one line to the /update POST handler in your sketch, before server.send(...):
    server.sendHeader("Access-Control-Allow-Origin", "*");
